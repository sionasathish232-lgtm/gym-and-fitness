BULKING SHAKE

  1. Liquid Base: 350 ml full-cream milk (or oat milk)
  2. Protein: 1-2 scoops whey protein powder (vanilla or chocolate)
  3. Carbs: 1 large banana + 60g rolled oats (blend the oats dry first)
  4. Fats: 2 tbsp peanut butter
  5. Bonus Calories: 1 tbsp honey or 1 tbsp chia seeds

weight loss shake

  • Liquid Base: 250ml unsweetened almond milk (only 30 calories)
  • Protein: 1 scoop vanilla whey or plant protein powder
  • Fiber & Carbs: 150g frozen strawberries or mixed berries
  • Satiety Booster: 1 tbsp chia seeds (helps thicken the shake and keep you full)
  • Stats: ~28g Protein | ~25g Carbs | ~6g Fat | ~270 Calories
